Why ShelfLife?

Good food is often thrown away simply because we are unsure how long it lasts or how to store it. ShelfLife is a student project that brings shelf-life estimates, storage advice and freshness signs together in one place, supporting responsible consumption (SDG 12) and reducing waste of food that others could benefit from (SDG 2).
