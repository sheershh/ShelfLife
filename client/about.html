About ShelfLife

A simple guide to how long food lasts and how to make it last longer.

What is ShelfLife?

ShelfLife is a website that shows general shelf-life estimates, storage advice, freshness stages and warning signs for everyday foods, along with simple ideas to use food up.

Why food waste is a problem

Food that is thrown away also wastes the water, energy, land and effort used to grow, transport and store it. Much of it is discarded at home simply because people are unsure about it.

Why storage awareness matters

Storing food in the right place, at the right temperature and in the right container can keep it fresh for longer, and knowing the warning signs helps people make careful decisions.

How ShelfLife helps

You can search foods, compare storage conditions, learn freshness stages, save foods you want to keep track of and find ways to use food before it spoils.
