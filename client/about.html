What it can't do.

ShelfLife judges how food looks, not whether it is safe. A photo can't show smell, temperature history or bacteria, and food can be unsafe without looking spoiled. Use it alongside the label and your own senses. When in doubt, throw it out.

Shelf-life figures are general estimates. Visible mould on soft food always means "throw it out", whatever the photo looks like otherwise.
